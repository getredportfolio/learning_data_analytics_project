Education generates substantial administrative and learning-related data, collected by educational institutions and local education authorities, including records of attendance, learner engagement, and academic performance. When analysed ethically and responsibly, these data can support the identification of performance patterns and inform evidence-based educational interventions.

The dataset student_performance.csv contains anonymised records representing demographic, social, and academic attributes of students. It is adapted from the publicly available UCI Student Performance Dataset and has been modified for educational and research training purposes within this module. All data are fully anonymised and used in compliance with ethical standards for secondary data analysis. No personally identifiable information (PII) is included, and the dataset is employed solely for teaching, learning, and assessment activities in a controlled academic context.

Two major issues in EDM are examined in this project: (1) Performance Drivers and (2) At-Risk Students. A structured analytical framework that combines exploratory analysis, predictive modelling, and literature-informed interpretation was created using an anonymised multi-feature educational dataset to assist evidence-based decision-making and individualised educational interventions.
